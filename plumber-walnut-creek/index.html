Water where it should not be: your first five minutes

Do this first

Whatever the source, the order of operations is the same in a house, a condo or a shop.

  • Close the nearest shutoff, or the main for your unit or building.
  • Switch off the water heater if it is the source, and kill power to wet areas only if the breaker is dry to reach.
  • In a condo or apartment, call the building manager or HOA so a shared riser can be closed.
  • Smell gas? Leave, call 911, then Pacific Gas and Electric at 1-800-743-5000 before calling us.
  • Call our emergency line and describe what is wet.

Postwar growth and older pipes

Walnut Creek's population jumped in the 1950s, and Broadway Plaza opened in 1951. Many houses and shops from that postwar growth period are still standing, and older buildings in general can have galvanized supply lines, cast iron drains or clay laterals near the end of their life.

If your home is that age, ask us about a camera look at the drain and a check of the shutoffs when we are on site anyway. Sections of rusted galvanized pipe can restrict flow long before they leak, and repiping is worth discussing when several fixtures show low flow together. A creek also runs through tunnels under downtown, so anything that means digging in a public street needs a city encroachment permit and a call to 811 first.

Before the plumber reaches your door

Water off, and tell the building

Close your unit or main valve. In a condo, notify the manager so they can shut a shared line.

Dry only, then power

Never stand in water near a panel or cord. Move electronics to a dry room if it is safe.

Open access

Reserve a parking spot, arrange lobby or gate entry, and clear the area under sinks and around the heater.

Call and describe

Give the unit number, the floor, and what is leaking. Ask for the price before work starts.

Hot water emergencies, from tanks to commercial heaters

A water heater failing at night is one of the most common reasons people call. Check these before we arrive.

  • Pooling water at the base: shut the cold supply valve above the tank.
  • No hot water at all: check the breaker or pilot, and note any error codes.
  • Lukewarm only: a failed element or thermostat is common in electric units.
  • Hot water smell or discolored tap: tell us when it started.

Houses and condos get water heater repair for dead elements, thermostats and relief valves. Shops, restaurants and offices also need commercial plumbing for grease interceptors, backflow devices and restroom lines, and we can plan the work around business hours.

Water quality and sewer service in Walnut Creek

Worth knowing

EBMUD reports hardness of 15 to 16 mg/L and a pH near 9.3 at its Walnut Creek treatment plant. That is very soft water, so scale seldom builds in heaters and a salt softener is usually unnecessary. Check the EBMUD water quality report for yourself.
