Reliez Valley lies in the northeastern part of Lafayette, where homes sit both in the hills and on the valley floor. Reliez Creek runs through it, and Briones Regional Park lies at the creek's source. If a water heater, drain or supply line at your Reliez Valley home needs attention, this page covers what to check first.

We take plumbing calls from Reliez Valley and the rest of Lafayette. We cannot quote a timing in advance, so ask what to expect for your street when you call.

A creek, a park boundary and a mix of homes

Reliez Creek is about four miles long. It begins as several intermittent streams on the east side of Lafayette Ridge, inside Briones Regional Park, then enters Reliez Valley and becomes a suburban creek. It is a tributary of Las Trampas Creek and part of the Walnut Creek watershed, which our Walnut Creek page covers from the city side. Along the way it passes through the grounds of Acalanes High School.

Local real-estate profiles describe the area as a mix of traditional neighborhoods, suburban courts, gated developments and larger estates, with Baywood, a gated development, backing onto Briones. For your plumbing the useful point is simple. Homes on slopes and homes on the flat have different problems, and gated or shared roads can affect how a crew reaches you, so mention both when you call.

Water heaters need earthquake bracing

California law

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires water heaters to be braced or anchored against earthquake movement. If you are replacing a water heater, ask that the strapping be part of the job. It helps keep the tank upright and the gas and water connections intact. See water heater replacement for that work. Water heater repair is the first call when the unit is leaking or not heating.

  • Water pooling at the base can mean the tank has failed.
  • No hot water at all can mean a burner, element or thermostat fault.
  • A rumbling or popping tank can mean sediment.

Very soft water changes the softener question

EBMUD reports water from its Lafayette treatment plant at 16 mg/L of hardness, about 0.9 grains per gallon. That is very soft. For most households this means a salt-based softener is usually not needed for hardness, and scale buildup in a tank or fixture is typically low.

If you want a filter, treat that as a taste decision. Test your tap and read the EBMUD annual water quality report before you buy equipment. EBMUD also uses chloramine, so anyone keeping fish needs a chloramine-safe conditioner.

What to do when water is somewhere it should not be

  1. Close the main shutoff, or the valve at the fixture if only one is leaking.
  2. Turn off the power to the water heater if the tank is leaking.
  3. Move belongings and towels away from the wet area.
  4. Check the meter with all water off to see whether the dial still moves.
  5. Call and tell us what you see and where.
