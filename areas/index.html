Lafayette itself: ZIP 94549

Lafayette has a single ZIP, 94549, and sits between Walnut Creek to the east, Moraga to the south and Orinda to the west. Together Orinda, Lafayette and Moraga are known as Lamorinda, so many homeowners in these towns share the same water and sewer providers.

Downtown runs along Mt. Diablo Boulevard, where shops and offices mean commercial plumbing calls are common. Moraga Road leads south toward Moraga and passes La Fiesta Square. Neighborhoods such as Burton Valley and Happy Valley are residential, and the terrain is hilly, which can complicate buried line access.

EBMUD supplies the water and Central San handles the sewers. EBMUD's Lafayette water is very soft, so hardness is rarely a cause of scale here. For sewers, the property owner owns the full lateral to the main, so a backup in a Lafayette home often starts a sewer line cleaning call.

How dispatch from Lafayette works

All calls reach the same 24/7 line. A dispatcher asks about the problem, takes your address and lines up a licensed plumber. If the problem is an active flood or sewage backup, it goes to the front of the schedule.

Because we dispatch from Lafayette, drive time to neighboring towns depends on the road. Highway 24 backs up at commute hours, and Moraga Road and Mt. Diablo Boulevard slow down with local traffic. We do not make fixed promises about timing, but the dispatcher will tell you honestly what to expect once your address is known.

While you wait, shut off the main valve and keep people clear of wet areas. A burst pipe or a hidden leak does the most damage in the first hour, so those steps matter more than anything else you can do. The same code, the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), applies in every town on this page.
