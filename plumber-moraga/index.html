Clogged and backing up: drains and sewer lines in Moraga

A slow drain is annoying. A drain that backs up into a tub or floor drain is an emergency. Here is what to do tonight.

  1. Stop all water use, including the washing machine and dishwasher.
  2. Check whether only one fixture is slow or several at once. Several at once points at the main line.
  3. Locate the cleanout, usually a capped pipe outside near the house. Leave the cap on if wastewater is near the top.
  4. Keep family and pets away from any standing wastewater, and wash hands after touching it.
  5. Call us. A single sink is a job for drain cleaning. A main-line problem may need a camera and a hydro jet, or if the pipe has cracked or collapsed, sewer line repair.

Sewer rules every Moraga owner should know

Worth knowing

Central San serves Moraga sewer. The property owner owns the lateral from the house all the way to the district main, even where it runs under the street. Root intrusion is the top cause of backups, so a pipe near large trees deserves a camera look before trouble starts.
