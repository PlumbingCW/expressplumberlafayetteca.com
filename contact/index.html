What to have ready when you call

A few details let us line up the right plumber quickly. None of this needs to be perfect, so call even if you only know some of it.

  • The address where the problem is, including the town and ZIP code.
  • What you see or hear: running water, a stain on a ceiling, a gurgling drain, no hot water.
  • Whether the water is still flowing and whether you found the main shutoff valve.
  • Which fixtures are affected. One slow sink points to a different cause than every drain in the house backing up.
  • Whether you rent or own, and if you rent, whether the landlord or property manager has been told.
  • The age and type of the water heater if that is the problem, and anything printed on its label.
  • A good callback number and a safe place for the plumber to park or enter.

Hours and how we handle your call

Dispatch is open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. That includes nights, weekends and holidays, because pipes do not fail on a schedule. Same-day appointments are offered when available for problems that are urgent but not an active flood.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. We tell you what we are sending a plumber to look at, and the plumber gives you a clear price before work starts. If the job turns out to be bigger than it sounded, the plumber explains why before anything changes.

Drive time depends on traffic and on where our plumbers are when you call, so we explain what is realistic for your address on the phone.
